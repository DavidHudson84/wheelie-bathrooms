1. Overview
Wheelie Bathrooms ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly, in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you interact with us—whether that’s through our website, phone, email, or in person.
2. What Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of personal information:
Contact details – Name, phone number, email address, and delivery/installation address
Booking and service details – Dates, times, and type of bathroom or hire service requested
Payment information – Transaction details (processed securely via our payment provider)
Website usage data – IP address, browser type, pages visited, and how you use our site
Any information you choose to provide – For example, through contact forms or service enquiries
3. How We Collect Your Information
We may collect your personal information when you:
Make an enquiry via our website, phone, or email
Book or hire one of our bathrooms
Subscribe to our newsletter or marketing updates
Interact with us on social media
Visit our website (via cookies or similar tracking technologies)
4. How We Use Your Information
We use your personal information to:
Provide, deliver, and install our bathroom hire services
Manage bookings, payments, and customer support
Communicate with you about your booking or enquiry
Improve our products, services, and website experience
Send relevant offers, promotions, or updates (if you’ve opted in)
Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
5. Sharing Your Information
We will never sell your personal information. We may share your details only when necessary to:
Trusted service providers (e.g., payment processors, delivery/installation partners)
Our professional advisers (e.g., accountants, legal advisers)
Authorities or regulators, if required by law
6. Cookies and Website Tracking
Our website uses cookies to:
Improve your browsing experience
Analyse site traffic and performance
Deliver relevant marketing through trusted partners
You can disable cookies via your browser settings, but this may affect how our site works for you.
7. Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, loss, unauthorised access, or disclosure. However, no online system can be guaranteed 100% secure.
